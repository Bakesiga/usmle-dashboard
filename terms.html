Terms of use

Last updated 3 November 2026.

This site is the study dashboard for Allan Bakesiga's USMLE Step 1 class. Using it means agreeing to what follows. It is short because the arrangement is simple.

Who it is for

Students enrolled on the course. Access is given to one person, by email address, and it is not to be shared. Signing in on behalf of someone else, or passing your access to someone who has not enrolled, ends it.

The materials

Recordings, notes, question maps and practice papers are provided for your own study while you are on the course. Do not copy, re-upload, resell or circulate them. Some of the material on the course is published by other people and remains theirs; the course gives you access to it, it does not give you rights over it.

Payment and enrolment

The course is paid for by the month, and what is open to you follows what has been paid for. Arrangements are made directly with Allan and are not handled on this site.

What this site is not

It is a study aid, not the examination, and not medical advice. Practice scores are an indication of where you are and nothing more. No result here predicts or guarantees a Step 1 outcome.

Availability

It is a small site run by one person alongside clinical work. It may be unavailable at times, features may change, and a timed sitting depends on your own device and connection. Keep your own record of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Ending access

You can stop at any time. Access may also be ended if the course ends, if payment stops, or if the terms above are broken.

Your information

What is collected and what happens to it is set out on the privacy page.
