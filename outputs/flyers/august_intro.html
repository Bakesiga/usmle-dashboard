The United States Medical Licensing Examination is the exam every physician must pass to be licensed in the United States. For an international medical graduate, it is the single gateway to a US residency and a career practicing medicine in America.

01What it is

A standardised, multi-part examination that tests whether a doctor can apply medical knowledge and clinical skills to safe, effective patient care, not just recall facts. Every physician who wants to practise in the US sits it, graduates of US and international medical schools alike.

02The three steps
Step 1  ·  Foundational sciences
Anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology and the rest, and how they explain disease.

Step 2 CK  ·  Clinical knowledge

Step 3  ·  Clinical practice
Readiness to manage patients independently. Usually taken during residency.
In residency
03Why it matters

For an international graduate, passing the USMLE is what earns ECFMG certification and makes you eligible for the Match, the system that places doctors into US residency programs. Put simply: no USMLE, no US residency.

04What is involved
The study

Months of structured work: First Aid alongside a large question bank, reviewed daily, then a full-day exam at a test centre.

The wider path

ECFMG certification, ERAS applications, interviews and finally the Match into residency.

It is a long road.
You should not walk it alone.
