The class runs on a repeating cycle, so join at any point, every module comes back around and nothing is ever missed.
How it runs
  • Live on Zoom every day, Monday to Sunday, 2 hours 30 minutes (10 pm EST / 5 am EAT).
  • Content review, then a walkthrough of exam-style questions.
  • Every class recorded on your dashboard to review any time.
  • A WhatsApp group for daily reminders and community.
  • A private one-on-one office hour with Allan each month.
  • We walk you through the rest of the road to the Match: Step 2 CK, ECFMG certification, US clinical experience, ERAS and letters of recommendation, interviews, and the visa path (J1 / H1B).
