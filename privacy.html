Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

This site is the private study dashboard for Allan Bakesiga's USMLE Step 1 class. It is used only by students enrolled on that course. This page says plainly what it collects, where that goes, and what it does not do.

Who runs it

Allan Bakesiga, allanbakesiga@gmail.com. There is no company behind it and no third party involved in running it.

What is collected when you sign in

Signing in uses Google. Google passes this site your email address, your name and your profile picture. The email address is checked against a list of enrolled students; if it is not on that list, nothing opens.

That information is kept in your own browser so you stay signed in. It is not sent to any server belonging to this site, because this site has no server: the pages are static files served by GitHub Pages.

Permission to read your Google Drive

When you sit a practice paper, the page asks for read only access to Google Drive. It is used for exactly one thing: to open the question paper from the class Drive folder and draw it beside your answer sheet, so the paper and the question stay in step.

  • It can only read. It cannot create, change or delete anything.
  • It opens the class papers and nothing else. Your own files are never listed, opened or examined.
  • The paper is held in your browser's local storage so later blocks start quickly and a dropped connection does not interrupt a sitting. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The paper then opens in Google's own viewer instead.

What you send in

When you send a practice result, what goes with it is: which form you sat, your email address and name, which questions you got wrong, and, from the timed player, the letters you chose, how long you spent on each question and which questions you flagged. It lands in a Google Sheet that Allan owns, and it is used to teach the corrections classes.

Anything you type into a check-in or feedback form on the site goes the same way, into a Google Sheet Allan owns.

What is kept on your device

Your sign-in, a half finished sitting so you can stop at a break and come back, and small preferences such as whether you left the lab values panel open. Clearing your browser data clears all of it, and a sitting in progress would be lost with it.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts following you around.
  • No cookies beyond what Google's own sign-in sets.
  • Nothing read from your Drive beyond the class papers.

How long it is kept

Your allowlist entry and your results stay for as long as you are enrolled and while the cohort's records are useful. Ask and they will be deleted.

Asking for your data, or its removal

Email allanbakesiga@gmail.com and say what you want. You can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for corrections, or ask for all of it to be deleted.

Where things are stored

Google Sheets and Google Drive, under Allan's Google account, and GitHub Pages for the pages themselves. Both are operated by companies in the United States, so your information is handled there.

Questions about any of this go to allanbakesiga@gmail.com.
