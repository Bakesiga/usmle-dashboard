About the class

One class, every day, until the exam is not frightening any more.

We meet live every day and work through the whole of First Aid in order, system by system, then answer exam style questions on the same topic while it is still fresh.

The class is live so you can interrupt, and questions are worked out loud so you hear the reasoning rather than just the answer. Most of what goes wrong on Step 1 is not knowledge, it is how a stem gets read, and that only improves out loud. Use the Raise Hand button. The class is better when you interrupt.

Finding your way around

  • Sessions holds every recording, grouped by system and sub-topic.
  • Progress shows how much of each system you have covered and which block we are in now.
  • Extra resources has the material that goes alongside the classes.
  • Office hours is where you book one to one time with me.

The things worth knowing

  • Every class is recorded and goes up here the same night, so missing one costs you nothing.
  • Registration is per month. Your Zoom link works for every session in that month.
  • Recordings are shared to the email you enrolled with. If one will not open, that is usually the reason.
  • Your subscription runs month to month and is due at the start of each month.

Taught by Dr. Allan Bakesiga, MBChB, MSc, a neurology resident who sat these exams and now teaches them daily.
